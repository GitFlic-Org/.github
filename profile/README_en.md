We present to you *GitFlic* - a Russian platform for developers.

GitFlic completed its Beta-testing stage in October 2021 and continues to evolve. We invite you to become our users and friends =)

### Share your opinion.

We would be grateful if you provide feedback on using our service: point out errors, shortcomings, and share ideas for improving GitFlic. You can record them in this [project](https://gitflic.ru/project/gitflic/gitflic/issue).

We will respond to all your suggestions; nothing will be left unattended.

You can report any issues you find with the service in this project. Use the following template to describe an issue:

* Clear title
* Steps to reproduce the error (you can add an image)
* Actual result
* Expected result

### Follow the updates.

We, the development team, share the results of our work. All current changes to the service are listed in the [release notes](https://gitflic.ru/project/gitflic/gitflic/file?file=release).

### Explore the service capabilities.

If you have questions about how a particular feature works in the service, please refer to the GitFlic documentation. It describes all the functionality of the service, and unclear points are regularly rephrased in more detail. The document is available at [https://docs.gitflic.ru](https://docs.gitflic.ru)

Thank you for your participation and contribution!

Sincerely, the GitFlic team
